Guardian Angel Donor Program

Become a Heavenly Paws Guardian Angel

Help WAGS spread pawsitivity and help Heavenly Paws serve pets, children, families, shelters, and rescues throughout our community.

Heavenly Paws Guardian Angels are caring individuals, families, churches, civic groups, and community supporters who help turn kindness into action. Guardian Angel gifts support the WAGS Community Outreach Initiative and the Paws for Purpose Initiative. Donors may choose the initiative they would like to support, divide their gift between both, or allow Heavenly Paws to use their gift where it is needed most.

Why It Matters

Guardian Angels Make the Mission Possible

WAGS was created to be more than a mascot. As the Heavenly Paws Community Ambassador, he brings children, families, pet lovers, businesses, and community organizations together through kindness, responsible pet ownership, service, and fun. Through Paws for Purpose, Heavenly Paws also works alongside shelters and rescues to support adoptable pets, new pet families, and animals in need. Guardian Angel gifts provide the supplies, educational materials, equipment, transportation, and resources needed to make both initiatives possible.

Two Ways to Make a Difference

Where Guardian Angel Gifts Go

WAGS Serving Children & Families

WAGS Community Outreach Initiative

The WAGS Community Outreach Initiative helps WAGS serve children, families, schools, churches, businesses, and community organizations throughout Madison County. Guardian Angel gifts may help provide:

  • WAGS school, church, daycare, and community visits
  • WAG Swag Bags and children's giveaways
  • Kindness cards, stickers, coloring pages, and activity sheets
  • Pet education and responsible pet ownership materials
  • WAGS Kids Club activities and service projects
  • Community events and special appearances
  • WAGS equipment, costume care, displays, and transportation
Hope for Shelters, Rescues & Adoptable Pets

Paws for Purpose Initiative

Paws for Purpose is Heavenly Paws' give-back initiative supporting local shelters, rescues, adoptable pets, and new pet families. Guardian Angel gifts may help provide:

  • Adoption Welcome WAGS Bags
  • Shelter and rescue supply drives
  • Adoption events and pet education
  • Shelter improvement and service projects
  • Equipment and supplies requested by shelters or rescues
  • Adoption photo opportunities and special projects
  • Resources that help adoptable pets and their new families

Dependable Support

Monthly Guardian Angel

Monthly Guardian Angels provide dependable support that helps Heavenly Paws plan future outreach, children's activities, shelter and rescue projects, and community service opportunities.

Become a Monthly Guardian Angel

Give any amount you choose. Suggested gifts: $25 · $50 · $100 · $250 · or a custom amount.

Your gift may help provide outreach materials, children's activities, adoption welcome bags, service-project supplies, equipment, or other resources needed to carry out the Heavenly Paws mission.

Give a Gift with Special Meaning

A Guardian Angel gift may be made in honor or memory of a special person or beloved pet. When requested, Heavenly Paws may send a notification letting someone know that a meaningful gift was made — the gift amount is never included.

Celebrating Our Guardian Angels

With permission, Guardian Angels may be recognized on the Heavenly Paws website, social media, newsletters, special event materials, or future Guardian Angel recognition displays. Recognition is always optional — you may also choose to keep your gift anonymous.
