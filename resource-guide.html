Welcome to the Heavenly Paws Resource Guide!

Your go-to directory for discovering trusted local businesses, services, and resources that help you give your pets the best care possible. From veterinarians and groomers to boarding facilities, pet boutiques, trainers, and more, we're connecting pet owners with businesses that support happy, healthy pets throughout Central Mississippi.

You'll also find our Heavenly Paws Paw Partners featured here — businesses that support our mission of building a stronger, more connected pet-loving community. Whether you're looking for your pet's next favorite groomer or discovering a new local business to support, we hope this guide makes finding the right resources easier.

Explore local. Support small. Love your pets a little more.

The Directory Is Growing

We're building out the full listing of trusted local businesses and resources for Central Mississippi pet owners. Check back soon — or if you're a local business that would like to be featured, join our PAW Partner Network.
