Guardian Angel Donor Program

Thank You, Guardian Angel!

Thank you for becoming a Heavenly Paws Guardian Angel. Your generosity will help support the WAGS Community Outreach Initiative, the Paws for Purpose Initiative, or the area of impact you selected.

Because of your kindness, Heavenly Paws can encourage children and families, support shelters and rescues, help pets in need, and create meaningful service opportunities throughout our community. We are grateful to have you as part of the Heavenly Paws mission.

One paw. One person. One good deed at a time.
