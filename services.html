Our Story

Loving Care When You're Not There

Heavenly Paws was founded on a simple belief: every pet deserves to feel safe, loved, and at home — even when their owners are away. We are a locally owned, family-run service built on trust, transparency, and a genuine love for animals.

Whether you need daily dog walks, in-home sitting, or overnight boarding, we tailor every visit to your pet's individual personality, routine, and needs. No cookie-cutter care — ever.
