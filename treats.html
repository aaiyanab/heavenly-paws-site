The Journey

OUR STORY

Heavenly Paws Treats began with a wag and a walk.

It all started when I launched Heavenly Paws Pet Care Services, offering pet sitting and dog walking to families in our community. Through countless tail wags, paw shakes, and heartfelt conversations, I kept hearing the same thing from pet loving parents—they were frustrated by the lack of truly natural, wholesome pet treats available in stores.

So I went from walking pups to whisking up treats in my kitchen, determined to make snacks that were both healthy and heart-made. Today, every treat is crafted with simple, natural ingredients and lots of love. Because our pets deserve the very best.

From my heart to your home, thank you for trusting Heavenly Paws with your furry family.

Love, Libbie
