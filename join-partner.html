Paw Partner Network

Become a Paw Partner

For local businesses that support pets, pet owners, and community connection. Sign up free and be recognized — or go Featured for spotlights, promotions, and event opportunities.

We created the PAW Partner Network to build connections between our members and local businesses. Our goal is simple: encourage pet owners to shop local with our PAW Partners. Your business does not need to sell pet products or allow pets inside to participate.

⭐ Featured PAW Partner

$149 / YEAR — FOUNDING RATE

At the founding rate of $149 through December 31, 2026, receive all free benefits plus enhanced visibility, newsletter and social media business spotlights, priority vendor opportunities, and a WAGS Business Celebration. We also distribute your supplied business information and promotions in our WAG Bags.

Both partner levels include a PAW Partner Business Guide listing to help members find and support your business. Pet-friendly locations will be identified so members know where their pets are welcome.
